# Privacy Policy

## What TradeNote stores
Your job logs, photos, materials, client messages and transcripts are saved on this phone. Your contractor name, company and phone number are saved on this phone and used only to sign your client messages.

## Voice memos and notes
When you tap Stop & Process or Process notes, the recording or text is sent over an encrypted connection to TradeNote's server, which uses AI services to transcribe it and write the job log. Recordings are deleted from your phone once they are processed and are not kept on our server.

## Your account
TradeNote creates an anonymous account for this phone so only you can use its processing. It holds no name, email or contacts.

## Sharing
We never sell your data or use it for advertising. A client message leaves the app only when you copy or share it yourself.

## Deleting your data
Settings > Delete Account & Data removes your account and every job log, photo and transcript from this phone at once.
